JustSplit

Privacy policy

Effective 26 October 2026

JustSplit is a small, free app for splitting shared expenses with friends. It keeps track of who paid for what and who owes whom. This page explains what it stores and why.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your name, email address and profile photo. If you sign in with Google, these come from your Google account; otherwise you type them in.
  • What you put into groups. Group names, the people in them, expenses, amounts, notes and settlements. If you add someone by email, that email is stored so the seat can pass to them when they sign up.
  • Password recovery answers, if you set them. They are hashed before they are stored, so nobody, including us, can read them back.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and we do not collect your location or contacts.

Google user data

Sign in with Google asks only for your basic profile: name, email address and profile photo. JustSplit uses them to create your account and to show you to the people in your groups, and for nothing else. JustSplit's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell this data, never use it for advertising, and never pass it to anyone except as described below.

Who can see it

  • People in a group with you see your name, your photo and that group's expenses. Nobody outside a group can see anything in it.
  • Signed-in JustSplit users can find you by your name, or by your exact email address, when adding people to a group. Results show your name, your photo and a partly hidden email (like m•••@gmail.com).

Where it is stored

Data is held by Supabase in its Mumbai, India region, with access rules on every table so each person can reach only their own account and their own groups. The site itself is served by GitHub Pages. Your browser keeps your sign-in session and your last open group on your device.

Deleting your data

You can delete any group you created from inside the app, which removes its expenses too. To delete your whole account, ask us using the contact below and we will remove it, with your profile and anything only you can see, within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/thisismaheshj/justsplit.
